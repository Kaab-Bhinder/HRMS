# Project Brief: Workforce — Premium HR Platform

## 1. Project Overview
**Workforce** is a high-fidelity, premium human resources platform specifically designed for banking and financial sector employees in Indonesia. The app prioritizes a "banking-grade" aesthetic, combining rigorous professional standards with an artistic, sophisticated user experience.

## 2. Brand Identity & Design Language
*   **Brand Personality:** Trustworthy, Secure, Sophisticated, Artistic, and High-Caliber.
*   **Visual Direction:** "Artistic Enterprise." A blend of sharp, functional UI components with organic, atmospheric backgrounds (glass/steel textures and ultra-modern architectural vistas).
*   **Primary Palette:** Deep Banking Blues (#1B4E8A, #2E6DB4) with Gold accents (#C0891C) and Silver highlights.
*   **Typography:** CONFIDENT (Inter / Inter Tight) for headings to instill trust; clean sans-serif for functional copy.
*   **Corner Radius:** Round Eight (8px) for a balanced, modern feel.

## 3. Key Visual Elements
*   **Backgrounds:**
    *   **Dark Mode:** Ultra-modern architectural perspectives of financial districts at night with light trails representing data flow.
    *   **Light Mode:** Ethereal, high-key abstract compositions of glass and steel architectural layers.
*   **Motion & Interactivity:**
    *   Custom GLSL shaders for organic, fluid transitions on entry.
    *   Staggered element reveals and parallax medallion effects.
    *   Tactile focus states with precise glows and rings.
